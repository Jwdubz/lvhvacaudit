What your site is costing you — LV Heating & Cooling —

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way someone with a dead AC in July would. The emergency line is up front and the service form asks for a day and time. The page itself is very slow, and a few details send mixed signals.

Checked October 16, 2026

What I found

01

The homepage is very slow to show its main image.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, your homepage's first content appeared at 5.3 seconds and its largest part, the big slider photo at the top, at 36.4 seconds. The page is about 10.0 MB. Someone with no AC is not going to wait that long before calling the next company.

02

You have two web addresses, and only one is the real site.

The live main site is www.lvhvacservice.com. lvhvacservice.com forwards to it, and so does the front page of lvhvacllc.net. But on lvhvacllc.net the inner pages I opened (About, Contact and Services) still load as full copies. Each carries a tag naming the lvhvacservice.com version as the original, which is the right setup, but it means two addresses are showing your site.

03

Google gets little to go on, and the first headline is the word “HOME”.

None of the 13 pages I saved has a page description. The homepage's first top-level heading is the single word “HOME”. The page titles each name a different part of town: Summerlin on About, Henderson on Blog, North Las Vegas on Services, Spring Valley on Air Conditioning and Enterprise on Contact.

04

The Contact page has a second phone number and an old year.

Every page shows (702) 323-9656. The Contact page also lists an alternate number, (702) 824-0905, that I found nowhere else, and its map pin is the general “Las Vegas, NV”, not your address at 6420 Cameron St Suite 206. The Contact page footer says © 2025.

05

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

Three buttons or links are too small to tap reliably, and one heading is out of order.

What's working

Your emergency offer is up front: “WE OFFER 24/7 EMERGENCY SERVICE. CLICK HERE TO BOOK NOW!” leads to a request form that asks for service address, zip, day and time. Your number (702) 323-9656 is a tap-to-call link on every page, and there is a contact form on every page, plus financing, reviews and maintenance-plan pages. For the question “HVAC maintenance plan Las Vegas” your page was in the answer.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether your forms and the “Instant HVAC Quote” tool work, and how fast your 24/7 line answers. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt allows every page. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Gap
    Business markup is generic

    The homepage markup is typed as an organization with name, website and image. There is no phone, address or opening hours.

  • Found
    Phone is a tap-to-call link

    (702) 323-9656 is a call link on every page I saved.

  • Gap
    No address in the homepage text

    The homepage text doesn't show your street address. The Contact page does: 6420 Cameron St Suite 206, Las Vegas, NV 89118.

  • Note
    Two addresses serve the site

    www.lvhvacservice.com is the main site. lvhvacservice.com and the lvhvacllc.net front page forward to it. About, Contact and Services on lvhvacllc.net load as copies with a tag naming the .com as the original, and its robots.txt and sitemap point to the .com.

  • Found
    Link to your Google review page

    The homepage links to your Google review page (g.page/r/…/review).

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 16, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Whether the “Instant HVAC Quote” tool, a third-party app on the Shop page, works. I didn't use it.
  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't ask those assistants myself, so the searches above are a stand-in.
  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.
  • Your Google listing itself: its reviews, photos, hours and phone number as Google shows them.
  • How your site looks and loads on a phone. I did not test a phone, so mobile is untested.

What I'd fix first

Speed up the top-of-page slider photo, retire the second address or forward every page of lvhvacllc.net to the main site, give each page a description and a headline other than “HOME”, put your street address on the homepage, and settle on one phone number. I can't promise where it will place you.
